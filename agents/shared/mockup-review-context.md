## What you review

You review HTML mockups in the New Feature workflow. Read spec.md, the before captures in your inputs and `mockups/*.html` under `specs/features/<feature-slug>/`. When you can, open the mockups in a browser and judge them by what they show. The mockups must follow dazl's existing Radix-based design system: its tokens, components and spacing.
